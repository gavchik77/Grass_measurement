Step 1a teaches the quadrat method. Step 1b teaches the plate meter method.

## Farm-case placement

The fixed farm case is now introduced immediately before **Step 5**, after students have already completed:

1. grass measurement by quadrat and plate meter;
2. animal grass demand;
3. Average Farm Cover;
4. grass-wedge interpretation.

The farm case then supplies the stocking rate, rotation, target cover, residual and extension-herd information needed for Steps 5–7.
